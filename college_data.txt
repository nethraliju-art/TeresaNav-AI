St.Teresa’s College
St. Teresa’s College, Autonomous is committed to enriching the lives of our students by empowering them. We provide holistic education that enables them to actively participate in community life. Womens’ education is a crucial factor that contributes to nation building and for the past Hundred years we have been educating young women from different strata of our society.
The concept of education needs to change keeping in mind the needs of the present generation of learners and we strive to equip them with the necessary skill sets to meet the different challenges that they may be required to meet.
Our Vision
St. Teresa’s College, as envisaged by our Foundress, Servant of God Mother Teresa of St. Rose of Lima promotes a life oriented education that empowers women through a humanizing and liberative process towards the creation of a civilization of love.
Our Mission
Our Mission is to empower women to transform Society.
OUR GOALS
St. Teresa’s, a premier Catholic Institution of higher education for women, provides university education in a Christian atmosphere. The College aims at the integral formation of “Intellectually creative, Emotionally mature, Morally responsible, Spiritually enlightened, Socially committed and Truly liberated” young women who will reposition themselves as self-reliant and responsible individuals, who would transform society.
The mutual trust and cordial relationship of the college community contributes to the effective realisation of our goal – an “empowered woman” who would fearlessly chart the journey of her life.
CORE VALUES
The distinctive characteristics of the institution are defined in the Mission statement with regard to the core values mentioned below
Addressing the needs of society
Preference for the economically challenged and marginalized
Transforming the self and society
Inculcating inter-religious harmony
Knowledge sharing with community and  effective social interventions
The students it seeks to serve
Holistic Education
Value Education and  Character Formation
Academic excellence
Inculcating intellectual curiosity and aptitude for research
Impart life skills such as decision making,  conflict resolution and team work
Institution’s Traditions and Value Orientation
Upholding an inclusive culture and broad vision for creating a “Civilization of Love”
Pursuit of Excellence & Maintenance of Quality
Inculcating Gospel values – truth, love, justice, peace,  integrity and faith
Facilitating interdependence and collaboration between the Management & Staff
Vision for the future
Open new gateways for transforming the institution into an excellent Women’s University
Develop new strategies to work together to ensure that the education offered maintains the highest quality
Offer an excellent grounding to the students that will equip them as highly sought-after graduates/post graduates and researchers
Empower Teresians to become the voice of truth and light with the power to change the world

Department of Bharathanatyam
Bharatanatyam is an extremely codified and schematized Asian Indian style of classical dance. That is loved and adored by numerous people who are passionate lovers of the art. The sole aim of this artform is to present a theme or feeling and to send an experience of it to the audience. The movements of Bharatanatyam are unique.
Traditionally, Bharatanatyam has been a solo dance that was performed exclusively by girls, and it expressed South Indian spiritual themes and religious ideas. Bharatanatyam is one of the most cherished and most popular classical Indian dance forms, not only within the country but also outside it. It is considered the epitome of Indian cultural expression.
Department of Bharathanatyam in
St.Teresa’s College
provides a comprehensive education in Bharathanatyam performance that helps to boost the career of each student. Bharatanatyam courses offered by the college are Bachelor of Bharathanatyam (2002 onwards) and MA Bharathanatyam (2012 onwards) which will establish foundations for creative and intellectual development and cultivate multiple approaches to dance and a career in it. The department offers a Bharatanatyam course in an environment where each student’s talent is nurtured and guided by the faculty. Thus, make your dream come true by acquiring a degree in the Bharatanatyam course.
Abhinayasangeetham and
Mohiniyattam
are complementary courses. Other Indian as well as western dances like salsa, ballroom, and hip-hop is also included in the theory syllabus.
Vision
“an uncompromising commitment to excellence” in the discipline of Bharathanatyam. To be a successful and sustainable community of elite dancers, well respected and recognized for their dance excellence and citizenship as well as developing the foundation necessary for those who aspire to pursue a career in Bharathanatyam.
Mission
Our mission is to instil a great love of dance, while inspiring self-confidence, discipline and respect of Bharathanatyam. Our goal is to give each individual dancer the tools they need to take dance as far as they dream to take it. We believe if you can dream it you can achieve it!We also believe that collegiate dance prepares young people for living meaningful lives, developing creative community impact and for career options in and beyond the dance performance field.

The Department of English
The history of the English Department is inextricably linked with that of the College. It has been one of the prominent departments of the College before and since its inception as an independent department in 1957, beginning with an undergraduate programme in English Language and Literature. Innovation has always been the hallmark of the Department and it now hosts Postgraduate, MPhil and PhD programmes in an ideal matrix for young scholars. With a team of dedicated faculty members headed by Dr Preeti Kumar, the Department continues to explore new avenues in its pursuit of excellence.
The learning environment is enhanced by the use of smart classrooms and efforts are taken to ensure that the learning process is innovative and progressive, giving due importance to problem-based learning. Assignments and other learning activities are set in line with this approach. Peer learning, remedial classes, mentoring and value education, student-centric participatory learning and proper grievance redressal further ensure the holistic development of students. In addition extension and literary activities are regularly conducted – internships, gender sensitization programmes, Alumni Lecture Series, faculty exchange programmes, visits to libraries of national repute, publication of manuscript magazines, the publication of an international peer-reviewed journal, the observance of student-author day, a Shakespeare Theatre Club and “Panache”, a literary fest.
Being a research centre, the Department engages in research work and ensures that workshops, conferences and seminars are conducted at regular intervals across the academic year. Minor projects and frequent visits of literary luminaries and experts ensure the students’ engagement in academics. The Department also provides a Certificate Course in Functional English for career enhancement. Coaching for the NET ( National Eligibility Test) is a recent initiative of the department. The involvement of the PTA has always been commendable, and their initiative in instituting prizes for the best undergraduate and postgraduate projects deserves special mention.
The Department has produced stalwarts in numerous fields – Writers, Editors, Heads of Departments, Chancellors, IFS officers and CEOs to mention a few. Distinguished alumnae who have made their mark in diverse places such as the New York Times, NUALS, Mahatma Gandhi University, the Government of Maharashtra, Harper Collins Publications, Scotland Bank, Google, and IBM among others, testify to the generations the Department has impacted.

Department of Economics
History & achievements – The Department of Economics
•The Department of Economics, started in the year 1937, is one of the oldest departments of St.Teresa’s College. The Department of Economics offers both an Undergraduate and Postgraduate course in Economics. While economics as a subsidiary subject for History main was started long back in 1927, the Bachelors programme with economics main was started in 1937 and masters programme in 1995. In 2010, the Department was elevated as a Research Centre of the Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam. The faculty comprises six permanent members, all holding doctoral degrees, including one faculty member with a postdoctoral degree.The Integrated M.A. Programme in Social Sciences (Economics) was sanctioned in 2021.The Department of Economics has bagged National Award , State Award and Chief Minister’s Award for innovations for the Project e-Jaalakam.
Mission – The Department of Economics
The department of Economics has around 225 students pursuing various courses and has produced good results and several ranks at the university level. Students play a prominent role and take the lead in organising many innovative, interdisciplinary action events/projects. Students are encouraged to learn beyond classroom through yearly budget analysis, stock exchange visit, participation in Grama Sabhas (Decentralised Planning Process of Local Governments) etc. A platform for hands on training in the form of internships in various institutions help students to apply their theoretical knowledge and prepare them to face challenges of job market. Volunteers also gain hands on experience in managing various facets of a social entrepreneurship unit- STEP ( Society of Teresians for Environment Protection) which include purchase of raw materials, inventory management, quality control, sales and marketing etc.
Vision
To mould Empowered, Empathetic and Environmentally sensitive Women Economists
To train students in economic analysis with particular emphasis on mathematical economics, create a research  aptitude for policy development and to empower them to become potential leaders and scholars.
To  inculcate a sense of social responsibility in them so that they become socially productive citizens.
Mission
To offer courses in  Applied Economics  so that it will enable the students to have a better understanding of current affairs in policy matters.
To facilitate comprehension of social, demographic, technological and business dynamics so that  students  can contribute intelligently to policy debate.
To create potential researchers who can enrich the nation through policy prescriptions.
To encourage women to make a positive impact on society through synergies within and outside the student community across  the college/region/national boundaries.
Message from HOD
We, at the Department of Economics, aim at moulding empowered, empathetic and environmentally sensitive women economists. We offer courses in Applied Economics that enhance research aptitude for policy design and empower students to become potential leaders and scholars. Recognized as a Research Centre of Mahatma Gandhi University, the department actively promotes quality research and academic excellence in diverse areas of economics.
Our Board of Studies, representing premier institutions such as Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Centre for Development Studies, National Institute of Technology Calicut, Ernst & Young, and the Centre for Budget Studies, Cochin University of Science and Technology has helped initiate significant changes in the pedagogy of teaching and learning. Our curriculum is focused on mathematical tools, application-oriented learning and economic policy analysis.
The department provides essential theoretical and practical training for pursuing careers in economics and management through internships, outreach programmes and research-oriented learning experiences. The department also takes pride in its students who have excelled in academics and professional careers, securing admissions and placements in prestigious institutions and organizations in India and abroad, including London School of Economics and Political Science.
Through academic excellence, research, and socially committed initiatives, the Department strives to nurture competent economists capable of contributing meaningfully to society and policy-making.

Department of French and Tourism Studies
Established in the year 1925, French was taught from the very inception of the college, first as second language and as main from 1928. Over the course of years, the Department of French and Tourism Studies has provided language skills to those students who had chosen French as a second language. In 2002, BA programme in French Language and Literature was re-introduced and thus became the first and only department in the state of Kerala to run an undergraduate program in French.
The department functions under the guidance of experienced and knowledgeable faculty members and also hosts French nationals qualified to teach at college level. The foreign teachers bring with them a wealth of knowledge and French culture and
traditions
. It is felt that a foreign addition to the teaching staff would enhance the quality and add depth to teaching. In addition, the department introduced a Master programme in Tourism and Travel Management in 2015.
MASTERS IN TOURISM AND TRAVEL MANAGEMENT
Vision
To develop a leading institute for research and development having courses producing professionally trained teachers with teaching competencies as well as values.
Mission
To provide quality education of global standards with latest technology, to inculcating values and patriotism and channelizing the energy of youth in the right path of service and sacrifice to the nation and society.
Message from HOD
A unique undergraduate program of its kind in the state of Kerala – to foster academic and personal excellence by creating; challenging and diverse learning opportunities.
As a foreign language department, we provide an ideal setting for language learning designed to serve individuals, industry as well as academic institutions carrying on the legacy of providing excellence in French language learning since 1925. As the learning of French language is of growing interest in today’s global society, the youth is well aware of its relevance. But unfortunately, in all universities of Kerala, it is taught only as an optional additional language. To make it more useful to the future generations, the department took the initiative of introducing it in the undergraduate level in 2002. Thus, a long cherished dream became a reality.
Our language program offers native faculty to equip the students with the relevant linguistic skills and expertise that they may require in a global work setting.
We truly believe that the ability to communicate in more than one language greatly affects relationships between groups and social cohesion. We are very proud of our commitment as a facilitator in French language learning and will continue to innovate our services to ensure that all those who join us enjoy a thoroughly enriching experience.

Department of History

college at that time
. Our department was then blessed with M.A History in 2013(Self-financing). At present, there are two permanent faculty and three guest faculty.

The University Grants Commission (UGC) had launched a scheme on 27 February 2014 for skills development based higher education as part of college/university education, leading to Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc.) degree with multiple entries and exit points. The B.Voc. programme is focused on universities and colleges providing undergraduate studies which would also incorporate specific job roles and their NOSs along with broad-based general education. This would enable the graduates completing B.Voc to make a meaningful participation in accelerating India’s economy by gaining appropriate employment, becoming entrepreneurs and creating appropriate knowledge.

Eligibility
: The minimum qualification required to be eligible to apply is a pass in the 10+2 examination or equivalent from any recognized Central or State Board or a course recognized as equivalent by the college.
The B.Voc Software Development course is designed in such a way that at the end of three years the student will be awarded a B.Voc. Degree and pursue the job roles of, Analyst, Application Maintenance Engineer, Deployment Engineer, Engineer Trainee, Junior Data Associate, Language Translator, QA Engineer etc.
At the end of the second year, they will be awarded Advanced Diploma and they can seek jobs as Business Process Outsourcer, Website Developer, Documentation support etc.
At the end of the First year, they will be awarded Diploma and they can seek the job as Data Entry Operators, Accountant Trainees etc.
Syllabus – B.Voc Software Development
The Syllabus is set by the faculties of Department of Computer Applications, St Teresa’s College [Autonomous], with the help of expert guidance from the Board of Studies of Department of Computer Applications. The Syllabus is approved by the Board of Studies, Department of Computer Applications, St Teresa’s College [Autonomous], Governing Council of, St Teresa’s College [Autonomous], Board of Studies (Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam), University Grants Commission (UGC), and NSDC (National Skill Development Corporation).
The B.Voc Software Development syllabus is a judicious mixture of General Education Component and Skill Component. The Curriculum includes training for Foreign Language (French and English), and a thorough practical training for Accounting Principles with Accounting Softwares. The Curriculum also equips students with thorough knowledge of Computer Hardware, Designing software such as Photoshop and Coreldraw. The Program includes Internship at all exit points and regular Industrial Visits.
Assessment
There are two Assessments for B.Voc. Software Development Program, External Assessment, and Internal Assessment.
External Assessment
The Skill component of the course will be assessed and certified by the IT & ITES Sector Skill Council.
The credits for the skill component will be awarded in terms of NSQF level certification.
Internal Assessment
Internal Assessment is carried out as per the Mahatma Gandhi University regulations.

Choose the ‘Write’ Career

Why Choose Us?
B.Voc. Applied Media Studies is a one-of-a-kind programme that enables students to pursue a career in the media industry. It is the first 3-year vocational graduate programme focusing exclusively on writing for Digital Media, Film, TV, Radio and Advertising in South India, offered by the Department of English and Centre for Research, St. Teresa’s College (Autonomous).
What this course has to offer?
B.Voc. in Applied Media Studies is a new programme that has adapted sector skill-based short-term, medium-term and long-term courses formulated under the National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF) notified by the UGC. This programme has a holistic approach to addressing the skill gap and promoting entrepreneurship. Its exclusive focus is on creating opportunities for young women to excel in different media domains such as Print, Film and Television, Radio, Advertising, and Social Media. The programme works to map core writing skills with job profiles in the media industry.
Our vision is to provide an immersive educational experience for learners by promoting collaborative and experiential learning. Therefore, we have revamped teaching, assessments, and classroom interactions to simulate aspects of the industry environment inside the campus. Through our massive alumni network, graduates of this course will have access to opportunities to expand their network, meet professionals in their field, and grow their skill sets. At St. Teresa’s, we have all the resources you need to excel-Come, Choose Your ‘Write’ Career.

Diploma in Healthcare Assistance
The Diploma in Health Care Assistance program is meticulously crafted to equip learners with a comprehensive understanding of the health needs of individuals, families, and communities, thereby fostering a holistic approach to care. Furthermore, it aims to cultivate a learning culture among students by modeling behavior conducive to serving the sick and needy within society. In addition, the program provides foundational knowledge in various subjects and complements this with practical sessions in Anatomy and Physiology, Community Health, Maternal and Child Health (including immunization), Nutrition, First Aid, Pain and Palliative Care, and Basic Nursing Care. Moreover, it enhances proficiency in the English language and communication skills, Office Management, Life Coping Skills, and Entrepreneurship, thereby ensuring well-rounded personal and professional growth. The program also facilitates a three-month internship in a renowned hospital, enabling students to apply theoretical knowledge in real-world settings; consequently, this prepares them for roles in both public and private healthcare sectors as assistants to qualified professionals. Not only does it provide fieldwork experience in palliative nursing and home care, but it also empowers graduates to excel in home care setups and healthcare institutions. Thus, the course ensures that learners are fully prepared to meet the demands of modern healthcare environments with confidence and competence.
Career Opportunities:
Nursing assistant in Hospitals.
Nursing assistant in old age homes.
Personal Home Nurses in Homes.
Formal Care givers for dementia/Parkinson’s/stroke patients both in Institutions and homes.
Palliative care giver.
Health workers in NGO’s.
Eligibility:
Plus Two Pass (Any Stream) Or Equivalent Qualification from recognized Board or University
No Age Limit

DIPLOMA IN INTERIOR AND EXTERIOR SPACE DESIGNING
Diploma in Interior and Exterior Space Designing
is a one-year (2 semesters) full time credit based programme. The main objective of this programme is to enable the students to carve a niche for themselves in the world of interior designing by equipping themselves with professional knowledge and skills to gain employment as Interior Designers or to freelance and thus provide them with professional opportunities. The programme empowers women with the holistic knowledge of designing Interior and exterior spaces with elan by providing an extensive training programme to pursue careers as Interior Designers, Architectural draftsperson, event and space designers and decorators. A unique feature of this programme is the interaction of the students with the industry through internships which will enhance their practical knowledge. In this trade we don’t just teach Interior Design, we encourage each and every student to access and nurture their own natural sense of flair and creativity. We also help them to know how they can create new ideas, thoughts and also to execute them in real form. In addition to this a candidate is entrusted to undertake project work, extracurricular activities and on job training to build up confidence.
Programme Structure
The learners gain knowledge in the preparation of various drawings for Interior designing, and learn to plan the design & position of furniture, fittings and fixtures within the space
They become competent enough to make small residential and commercial interior layouts with scheduled sizes of furniture & apply colour schemes.
The presentation skills both manual and computer based also will be taught.
Their ability to check, identify, analyse, and draw the Interior jobs and take it to a professional level also will be an asset to any employer.
In addition to the trade skills communication abilities in English, Entrepreneurship development, Basics of Office management, basic accounting and bookkeeping skills are also included in the curriculum.
The trainees also undergo two weeks project work each (one month) at the mid and end of the year which gives them more practical exposure and helps to build up confidence level.
